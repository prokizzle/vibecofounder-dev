# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2025-07-17*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** On-demand technical cofounder who gets vibe coders past the 80% wall and into production.
**What it does:** Nick Prokesch steps in as a senior engineer to handle the hard 20% that AI tools can't reliably produce — auth flows, payment infrastructure, database schema design, CI/CD, mobile deployment, App Store submission, and architecture review. He works alongside the founder, not as a black box: you review PRs, you stay in control.
**Product category:** Technical consulting / on-demand fractional CTO / technical cofounder-as-a-service
**Product type:** Service (consulting, project-based engagements)
**Business model:** Free 30-minute intro call → scoped project engagement. No retainers, no long-term lock-in. Likely hourly or fixed-project pricing.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Solo founders, indie developers, early-stage startups (pre-seed, seed) building with AI-assisted development tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, Bolt, v0, Lovable).
**Decision-makers:** The founder themselves — they're the builder and the buyer. Technical enough to ship a prototype with AI, not experienced enough to make it production-grade.
**Primary use case:** Turning an AI-generated prototype into a shipped, stable, production-ready product.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Fix what AI built before it breaks in production
- Get through App Store review
- Ship instead of adding infinite features
- Have an experienced engineer validate architecture decisions
**Use cases:**
- "My Stripe webhooks silently fail and I don't know why"
- "My iOS app keeps getting rejected from App Store review"
- "I've been two weeks from launch for three months"
- "Five users hit my app and the database melted"
- "I need someone to review my AI-generated architecture before I launch"

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude) are incredible for prototyping but fail at production-grade engineering. Vibe coders can build 80% of a product in a weekend, then get completely stuck on the last 20% — the part that actually matters for real users.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **More AI prompting** — hits the same wall, just with different wording. AI doesn't know what it doesn't know about production infrastructure.
- **Hiring a full-time engineer** — too expensive, too slow, too much overhead for a pre-revenue founder.
- **Traditional dev agencies** — minimum $50k engagements, 6-month timelines, pitch decks over problem-solving.
- **YouTube tutorials / docs** — not personalized, not architectural, doesn't debug your specific stack.

**What it costs them:**
- Months of delayed launch ("two weeks from launch" becomes a running joke)
- Users churning because the product breaks
- App Store rejections blocking distribution entirely
- Lost investor confidence when demos crash
- Personal burnout from debugging production issues they didn't build

**Emotional tension:**
- "I built this whole thing and now I can't finish it"
- "What if someone sees my auth is broken?"
- "I'm so close — why can't I just get past this?"
- Embarrassment at shipping something that breaks under load
- Fear that their "startup" is really just a fragile prototype

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Fractional CTOs, technical consultants, dev agencies — falls short because they speak enterprise, not indie. They pitch retainers, not outcomes. They don't understand AI-assisted workflows or vibe coder culture.

**Secondary:** More AI (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, Bolt, v0, Lovable) — falls short because the models can't reliably handle production auth, payments, database schema design, iOS provisioning, or App Store compliance. They don't have context on your specific stack's edge cases.

**Indirect:** Doing nothing / giving up — the founder stays stuck, the project dies in "almost done" purgatory. Hiring a full-time CTO — too expensive, too early, wrong stage.

**How each falls short for this audience:** Nobody else speaks directly to the vibe coder experience. The entire market either talks to "engineering leaders" or "CTOs" — not to the solo founder who built their MVP with Claude and a weekend of vibes.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Built specifically for vibe coders and AI-first founders — speaks their language, understands their stack, knows where AI fails
- Full-stack across platforms: iOS (Swift/SwiftUI), Android (Kotlin/Compose), TypeScript, Firebase
- Not a black box — founder reviews PRs, stays in the loop, learns from the process
- No retainers, no long-term commitments — scoped engagements with clear deliverables
- Free intro call that's actual problem-solving, not a sales pitch

**How we do it differently:** Instead of replacing the founder's work, we work alongside them. They stay in control of the codebase and product direction. We handle the specific hard things they can't solve with AI.

**Why that's better:** The founder ships faster without losing ownership, learns what production-grade looks like, and doesn't get locked into an expensive ongoing relationship they don't need.

**Why customers choose us:** Because they're tired of being stuck, tired of prompting, and ready to ship. They want someone who gets it — not someone who'll explain what a webhook is.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Can't I just keep prompting the AI?" | If that was working, you wouldn't be here. AI is great at known patterns, terrible at your specific edge cases in production. |
| "I can't afford a consultant." | What's the cost of not shipping? Three more months of "almost done" is three months of no users, no revenue, no feedback. A scoped engagement costs less than a month of full-time engineering salary. |
| "I don't want to lose control of my codebase." | You review every PR. You stay in the loop. I'm here to unblock you, not take over. When the engagement ends, you have a production-grade codebase you understand. |

**Anti-persona:** Founders who want someone to build the whole thing from scratch while they wait. Enterprise companies looking for staff augmentation. Developers who already have a senior engineering team.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** AI hits its limits — broken auth, failing payments, App Store rejections, database collapse under real load. Frustration with endless prompting. The gap between "looks great in a demo" and "actually works" becomes undeniable.

**Pull:** A senior engineer who ships — no retainer, no pitch deck, no 6-month engagement. Someone who understands their stack because they've worked with the same tools (Cursor, Firebase, Stripe, Vercel). Social proof from other vibe coders who got unstuck.

**Habit:** The DIY loop is addictive — "just one more prompt" feels like progress even when it's not. They've already sunk weeks or months into AI-assisted development. Handing the keys to someone else feels like admitting defeat.

**Anxiety:** "What if they're too expensive?" "What if they judge my AI-generated code?" "What if I lose control of my product?" "What if they don't actually understand my vision?" Fear of being sold to, not helped.
